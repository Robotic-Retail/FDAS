# BFP Consultation 

## Materials To Prepare Before BFP Consultation

- Rough renovation plans
- Rough floor plan of the warehouse and the proposed division
- Clarification of proposed operations
- Identification of handled materials and products
- Existing building plans, occupancy permits, or fire safety inspection certificates

## Matters To Discuss In BFP Consultation

- Occupany classifications
- Hazard categories
- Applicable (and maybe minimum) requirements and architectures for the fire detection and suppression systems and the fire safety plan according to proposed renovation and operations
    - Automatic or manual fire alarm system
- Smart home WiFi smoke detectors
- Custom circuit board for fire alarm control panel
- Custom interaction between FDAS and fire suppression system
- Solenoid valves for zone-specific water discharge
    - Existing approved systems for zone-specific localized water discharge aside from  conventional heat-activated sprinklers
- Supplementary early fire alert system using computer vision, but entirely separate from actual FDAS as it requires human verification
    - Could possibly emulate an addressable FDAS
